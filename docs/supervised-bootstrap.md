# Human supervised bootstrap, without changing inference

This candidate slice prepares two separate policy updates: ordinary action choice and offered-card selection ranking. It preserves recorded observations, source/action bindings, native acceptance, unknown masks and all original checkpoints. It does not promote a candidate or resume PPO. Coaching material is excluded.

`scripts/prepare-supervised-bootstrap.mjs <private-config> <private-plan> <fresh-output>` reads completed local human journals and the verified puzzle-answer review. It checks raw journal hashes, sequence and manifest pins, natural terminal closure, human attribution, observation hashes, exact feature re-encoding and semantic-to-native command resolution. It exports only human offer encodings and supervision, never generic receipt views or opponent actor observations. Private plans, journals, catalogs and datasets belong outside Git or under ignored `runs/`.

The private plan names training games, pinned puzzle review and pinned replay mapping proof. Fresh simulator seed blocks are predeclared before fitting; a new human game is not required. Games1–2 and all four puzzle answers are training/diagnostic material. They cannot be relabeled untouched holdouts, and arbitrary row splits from these games are not independent validation.

## Prompt masks and acceptable choices

Ordinary records have the native complete legal action list. Prompt records retain the original null joint-legality masks. The existing selection route ranks the complete offered card-option pool, then its unchanged driver forms bounded combinations and requires native preview before execution. Thus a demonstrated accepted card set can supervise **option ranking** without claiming every alternative combination was legal. This selection objective is not an on-policy action distribution and human records have no behavior log probabilities.

For ordinary explicit acceptable alternatives, the loss is negative log total probability of the accepted set. It does not force equal probability on both targets. For a recorded multi-card selection, ranking loss averages the log probabilities of the actually selected members. This encourages their ranking, without pretending to learn a normalized distribution over legal combinations. Exact imitation disagreement is not a strategic-error grade; unchosen choices are ungraded.

## Bounded candidate fit and fresh evaluation gate

The entry point is `python -m learning.bootstrap_bc --admission <receipt> --admission-sha256 <sha> --inspect`. Inspection checks dataset hashes, per-head coverage and the immutable predeclared simulator protocol **before importing torch or loading a checkpoint**. Missing or changed evaluation reservation blocks fitting; lack of another human demonstration does not.

Once admission qualifies, fitting also requires explicit `--ordinary-checkpoint`, `--selection-checkpoint`, `--model-root` and a fresh `--output` under an approved ignored model directory. The initial bound is at most 100 total alternating optimizer updates and 120 training seconds on CPU. The ordinary actor starts from the exact frozen update23 actor; the selection actor starts from its separately pinned current selection checkpoint. Core model source/contract guards remain unchanged. The output preserves the ordinary PPO wrapper, critic and stored PPO optimizer state, attributing BC updates separately; the selection output uses its existing checkpoint format. Inference architecture and the native validation route are unchanged.

These are training-material measurements, not generalization or strategic-error grades. It freezes the final two-head candidate against update23 for 32 independent seed blocks, each played from both seats. Two workers have a 90-minute hard wall budget, a 240-second game budget and a 600-command common draw cap. Shared harness failures invalidate the entire block and use the next of eight predeclared replacement blocks; attributable policy failures are forfeits. Ambiguous failures are never awarded as wins. Global exhaustion reports incomplete rather than extending the run.

The first eight completed blocks are an operational checkpoint, not an early strength claim or tuning opportunity. The planned final score averages win1/draw0.5/loss0 within each seed block. Incomplete or less decisive results remain inconclusive; 64 games are a proposed measurement budget, not a ranked-deployment gate. Any later candidate selection needs fresh confirmation seeds.
